# Group UX: body drag, title size, header toggle, space panning

> **Date** 2026-11-24 · **Kind** execution · **Status** final · **Base** `LG/dev` @ `d47f5ef` · **Plan** none (the user's report of 2026-11-23, relayed by the parent session)

The user reported three problems with groups in `apps/editor/editor_build.html`:

- the group "pin" covers the title and grows with zoom;
- a drag anywhere in a group moves it, and the space bar no longer pans;
- the user wants group title text sized by canvas scale, as an option that is on by default.

Each was reproduced in the browser with real drags, its cause found, and then fixed on `LG/dev_HMD_groups` with the defaults the user chose.

## Findings

- **The "pin" is the group header toggle** (mute/unmute the group's nodes), added in `d70da22` with the setting `LiteGraph.Group.HeaderToggle`. It is not the node pin.
  - It was sized from the title height in graph units, so it grew with zoom without limit: about 24 px at zoom 1, 60 px at zoom 2.5.
  - It was drawn as an opaque white disc.
  - The title wrapped across the full group width, so the title ran under it.
- **Body drag:** a press anywhere in a group started the group move. That is the original LiteGraph behaviour, carried onto the pointer-gesture API in `e068f09`. `ff67788` added Ctrl on the title band to move the group alone.
- **Space panning was broken by the keybinding registry** (`b3ea046`), not by the groups:
  - `KeybindingRegistry.fromEvent` turned the space key into the combo `" "`;
  - `normalize(" ")` threw "combo must be a non-empty string";
  - so `processKey` aborted before it set `dragging_canvas`.

  It failed on every space press, anywhere.

## Decisions (asked with AskUserQuestion, recommended option taken in each case)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Group body drag | The body pans the view, like empty canvas. The title bar moves the group (as in ComfyUI); Alt+drag on the body moves it; Ctrl on the title moves the group alone; Ctrl on the body box-selects. Setting `LiteGraph.Group.BodyDrag`: `pan` (default) or `move` (legacy). |
| Header toggle | Node-pin look and size (`LGraphGroup.opts.toggle_size` = 10 graph units), at most `toggle_max_px` = 14 px on screen, hidden below `toggle_min_px` = 6 px. Placed at the right end of the title band; the title is wrapped and clipped left of it. `LiteGraph.Group.HeaderToggle` stays on by default. |
| Title size | `LiteGraph.Group.TitleScale` (on): the title's on-screen size is kept between `LiteGraph.Group.TitleMinPx` (12) and `TitleMaxPx` (48). Off: the group's `font_size` at every zoom (legacy). The title band used for selection and title drags follows the drawn size. |

## Changes

- `feat:` group body drag rule, title size by zoom, capped header toggle, space panning:
  - `src/lgraphcanvas.js`:
    - `getGroupTitleFontSize`, `getGroupTitleHeight`, `isOverGroupTitle` and `groupPressMoves`;
    - `getGroupToggleRect` and `drawGroupToggle` reworked;
    - the title is clipped in `drawGroups`;
    - a press with space held starts `_dragCanvasGesture` before any hit test;
    - `_space_pan` and `_endSpacePan` handle keyup and canvas blur, and panning goes on while space is still held after a drag.
  - `src/keybindings.js`: the space key becomes `"space"`, and `normalize(" ")` is accepted.
  - `src/lgraphgroup.js`: the `opts.toggle_*` options.
  - `src/litegraph.js`: the four new settings, with tooltips.
  - `test/groups-ux.test.js`: 19 tests.
- `docs:` guide (Groups, settings table, default keys), `CHANGES.md` (Unreleased), the codebase map, the roadmap track row, this record.
- `build:` regenerated distributions.

## Left open

- **Placement of the CHANGES entries:** they are under "Unreleased" rather than the 0.18.0-dev.2 section, because the node pin track also put its entries there after that section was closed. Move them if this track should ship in dev.2.
- **The toggle disappears below about zoom 0.6** (6 px). A user who wants it there can lower `LGraphGroup.opts.toggle_min_px`.
- **Legacy space-hover panning** still runs through `processMouseMove`'s `dragging_canvas` branch, now only while space is held and no button is pressed. A press pans as a gesture.
